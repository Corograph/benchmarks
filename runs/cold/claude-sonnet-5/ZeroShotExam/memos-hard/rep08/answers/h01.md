**S3 presign runner** (`Runner.CheckAndPresign`, `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:47`) — re-generates presigned S3 GET URLs for attachments before they expire, so external attachment links don't go stale.

- **Component**: `server/runner/s3presign/runner.go` — iterates attachments whose S3 object payload's `LastPresignedTime` is more than 4 days old (URLs are presigned for 5 days via `internal/storage/s3/s3.go:79`) and re-presigns them (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:85-118`).
