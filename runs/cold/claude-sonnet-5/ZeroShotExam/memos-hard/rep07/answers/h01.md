**Component:** `s3presign.Runner` (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go`) — a background runner that re-presigns S3 object attachment URLs before they expire.

**Schedule:** Runs once immediately at startup, then on a ticker every 12 hours (`server/runner/s3presign/runner.go:27` `const runnerInterval = time.Hour * 12`). Each run skips any attachment whose presigned URL was refreshed within the last 4 days (URLs expire after 5 days), so stale-but-not-yet-expired links get refreshed with margin to spare (`runner.go:85-91`).
